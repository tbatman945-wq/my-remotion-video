# Background Music

Place background music files here:

- `ambient.mp3` - Looping ambient track for the video

## Requirements

- Format: MP3, WAV, or OGG
- Length: Should loop seamlessly or be longer than video duration (5s)
- Volume: Mixed at ~25% in code, ducks to ~8% during narration

## Sources

- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/)
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary/music)
- [Incompetech](https://incompetech.filmmusic.io/)
- Generate with AI: Suno, Udio, Stable Audio
