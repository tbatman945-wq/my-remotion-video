# Sound Effects for PS2 Winter Walk

Place these sound effect files in this folder:

## Required Files

| File | Description | Duration | Volume |
|------|-------------|----------|--------|
| `snow_step.mp3` | Crisp snow crunch footstep | ~0.3s | 0.6 |
| `wind_winter.mp3` | Howling winter wind (loop) | ~10-30s | 0.25 |
| `city_ambience.mp3` | Distant city sounds (loop) | ~10-30s | 0.15 |
| `breath_cold.mp3` | Visible breath exhale | ~1s | 0.4 |
| `jacket_rustle.mp3` | Clothing fabric movement | ~0.5s | 0.3 |

## Audio Specifications

- **Format**: MP3, WAV, or OGG
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit or 24-bit
- **Channels**: Mono (for footsteps/breath) or Stereo (for ambience)

## Sound Design Notes

### snow_step.mp3
- Sharp transient attack (crunch)
- Short decay
- High frequency content (2-8kHz)
- Slight variation per step (pitch ±5%)

### wind_winter.mp3
- Seamless loop point
- Low frequency rumble (50-200Hz)
- Occasional high-frequency gusts
- No distinct patterns

### city_ambience.mp3
- Very distant, muffled
- Occasional car pass-by
- Subtle electrical hum
- No recognizable speech

### breath_cold.mp3
- Soft exhale through mouth
- Condensation "puff" quality
- ~500ms duration
- Low volume, intimate

### jacket_rustle.mp3
- Nylon/polyester fabric sound
- Subtle, not distracting
- Short duration
- Random timing in composition
